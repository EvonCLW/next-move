A step-by-step tool for decisions that matter, from job offers and courses to dates and big purchases. Compare your options based on what actually matters to you.

    Your options

    What kind of decision is it? This sets the factors and examples you'll see.

    Deciding whether to do one thing?

    Your first instinct. You'll compare it with the analysis at the end.

    What matters to you

    Pick up to 7 factors. Fewer is better: choose the ones that would truly change your mind. If you add your own, phrase it so higher is better ("Affordable" rather than "Cost").

    Dealbreakers

    Is there a minimum you won't go below? Any option scoring under it will be ruled out, however good it is elsewhere.

    Your priorities

    Compare your factors two at a time. It's easier to say "growth matters more than salary" than to rate everything on its own. The app turns your answers into weights.

    Score your options

    Rate each option on each factor: 1 = poor, 5 = excellent. Some choices get better or worse over time, so rate the short and long term. The second score copies the first until you change it. For a "No" option, rate how things would be if you don't make the change.
